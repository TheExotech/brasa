Problem

MODIS is retiring, VIIRS sees 5–10× more hotspots — spliced raw, the record shows a fake fire surge

Argentina 2023 → 2024: raw detections jump +67 % (191k → 319k) while true burning barely moves (25.9k → 25.3k). Splice raw and every fire manager sees a false alarm. Argentina: +429 % bias. Philippines: +896 %.

Method

Filter → dedup to ~1 km cell-days → calibrate to Aqua → assemble → quantify uncertainty

1 FilterVegetation fires only, nominal/high confidence, per-platform outage detection.
2 NormaliseDeduplicate pixels to 1 km active-fire cell-days; removes the 375 m vs 1 km multiplicity.
3 CalibrateReference-satellite ratios over the stable 2012–2021 overlap, day/night seasonal, empirical-Bayes per cell.
4 AssembleTerra 2000 → Aqua → VIIRS → NOAA-20 continuity as S-NPP retires; MODIS-equivalent units.
5 Uncertainty90 % prediction intervals per converted month from leave-one-year-out residuals.

Same principle INPE uses for Brazil's official series, extended with cell-level empirical-Bayes multipliers, sensor-retirement continuity and published prediction intervals.

Impact and next steps

One fire record, whatever satellite saw it

  • Any country: one line in regions.json plus a pipeline run.
  • NOAA-21 nesting and automated monthly refresh for operational use.
  • Removes a sensor artefact that would otherwise read as a false alarm in early warning.
  • Stays valid after MODIS and S-NPP retire — the record does not break.
